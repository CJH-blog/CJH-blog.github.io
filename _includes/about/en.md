# What I've been building

For the past five years I've been working on the machinery behind online advertising — the part nobody sees but everybody pays for: real-time bidding, delivery controls, candidate retrieval, and ranking.

Most recently I spent two years at **ByteDance** on the Pangle / CSJ brand ads team, where I owned large chunks of the demand-side delivery path across six services. The work I'm proudest of there: redesigning frequency capping to fix underdelivery (cutting the frequency-data miss rate by 99%), consolidating half a billion near-duplicate campaign IDs into eleven million principal IDs, and squeezing pre-ranking inference to ~2.4× baseline throughput. I also got to experiment with using an AI agent to analyze the ad exchange codebase and automate performance work — cutting average CPU usage by half, and honestly the most fun I had all year.

Before that, I spent three years at **NetEase Youdao**, where I built a brand ads engine in Java and Spring Boot from scratch — role-based access, ad-slot scheduling, targeting, frequency capping — and watched it grow from ~RMB 1M to ~RMB 10M in daily ad spend. I also owned our RTB integrations and set up the GitLab CI/CD pipeline for Kubernetes deployments.
